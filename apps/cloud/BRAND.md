# CodeRocket brand

## Promise

**Frontend quality, cleared for launch.**

CodeRocket is direct, technical, and calm. It communicates evidence rather than synthetic scores.

## Mark

The mark is an abstract rocket and negative-space flame, tilted 45 degrees toward the upper-right. Use it without extra containers, shadows, gradients, or outlines.

- Minimum digital size: 16 px
- Preferred header size: 32 px
- Clear space: at least one quarter of the mark width on every side
- Do not rotate, stretch, add a detailed flame, or place the mark in a circle

## Color

- Space: `#09090B`
- Midnight: `#0B1020`
- Electric violet: `#7C5CFC`
- Signal cyan: `#22D3EE`
- White: `#FAFAFA`

The mark is normally white on Space, Electric violet on neutral backgrounds, or black in monochrome applications.

## Type

- Geist: interface, body copy, wordmark, and product headings
- Geist Mono: buttons, audit metadata, and technical labels
- Playfair Display: marketing display copy only
